About me

An engineer
in the field.

I’m a mechatronics engineer from the University of Cape Town, now supporting semiconductor equipment at STMicroelectronics in Catania. My work takes me from mechanical faults and electrical systems to software alarms, commissioning and production recovery.

I’m drawn to problems where software meets physical systems. My university projects explored LiDAR perception, autonomous robotics and embedded integration; my current role has taught me to diagnose complex machinery under real production pressure.
